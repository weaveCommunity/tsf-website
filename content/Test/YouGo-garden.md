The YouGo Garden consists of an array of raised beds framed in straw bales and filled with a high-carbon mix of by-products of the food system and landscape, fertilized with pee!  We have grown lush and delicious gardens for the past few years, using this evolving method that was invented right here at the Soil Factory.
