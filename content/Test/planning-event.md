So you have an approved event, what should you do next to bring it to the community?

1. **Contact the schedule coordinators** by emailing [soilfactoryevents@gmail.com](mailto:soilfactoryevents@gmail.com) with your proposed date and time for the event. Make sure your trained host(s) are also CC'd on this email.
2. **Once you have received a confirmation** from one of the scheduling coordinators, they will also place it on the public facing calendar. Please **provide a brief description (two to three sentences) of the event** that they can add to the calendar.
3. You will need to notify the newsetter of your event **by the Sunday two weeks prior to your event**. Email [soilfactorynewsletter@gmail.com](mailto:soilfactorynewsletter@gmail.com) with a brief description of your event, including an image [insert size limit here]. If the event is limited to a certain number of participants, you will also want to include a link to your registration page. **NOTE:** *Do not send events to the newsletter until after they have been cleared through the scheduling coordinators***.**
4. During your event, make sure to take a few **pictures** for the newsletter. 
5. Following the event, send **a brief description highlighting what happened** together with one or two photos [insert size limit here] to soilfactorynewsletter@gmail.com. This is due by the Sunday following your event.

Thank you for sharing your event with the Soil Factory community!
