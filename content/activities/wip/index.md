Since 2025, the Soil Factory has participated in the Watershed Internship Program (WIP), which is organized by Discover Cayuga Lake. In 2025, intern Ray Blegen supported the bale bed gardens and took the lead in building an EcoLu using a simple grid-beam technique.  In 2026, interns Makenna Dovel and Fisher St Clair helped with a major garden upgrades that included beautiful artwork. In 2026, interns Caleb LeBlanc and Kyleigh Tangen are continuing to provide beautiful gardens for the Soil Factory community.
